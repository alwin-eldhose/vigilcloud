Practice event handbook

These details are made up for the workshop. They do not describe this event.

Quiet room: Room Cedar on the second floor. Ask visitors to keep conversations
outside the room. They can use it whenever they need a quiet break.

Lost items: Bring found items to the welcome desk. A volunteer there records
what was found and where. Send anyone looking for an item to that desk.

Step-free entrance: Use the east entrance beside the courtyard. A lift inside
serves both floors.

Questions this handbook does not answer: Ask the coordinator at the welcome desk.
